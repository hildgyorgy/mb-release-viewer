MusiCards

MusicBrainz Release Viewer

About

MusiCards is a visual browser for MusicBrainz releases and artists. It focuses on album-based exploration and provides structured display for classical music.

How to use

  • Search by artist name to browse artists.
  • Type , release title with a starting comma to search for releases.
  • Use artist, release for combined search.
  • Scan CD barcodes using the camera.
  • Drag the cards up or down, or tap their top edge, to expand or collapse them.

Current version

Version 1.0 includes:

  • Release → Tracks → Artist navigation.
  • Barcode scanning.
  • Now Playing matching using Apple Music.
  • Classical music track structure display.

Privacy Policy

MusiCards does not collect, store, or share personal data.

The app stores recent searches and selected items locally on your device using system storage.

MusiCards connects to external services to retrieve music data:

  • MusicBrainz for music metadata.
  • Cover Art Archive for album artwork.

These services may process requests according to their own policies.
